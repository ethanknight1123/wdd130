What is Hollow Knight?

Hollow Knight is a 2d platformer game made by Team Cherry in 2017. The game is based around exploring the world of hallownest, which has been ravaged by a mysterious disease turning most of the inhabitants into midless zombies. A major mechanic of the game is the charm system. You have a certain number of charm notches, upgradable throughout the game, and each charm has a notch cost associated. Charms are found one by one as you explore the world of hallownest. Each charm has different synergies with other charms, and players are encouraged to customize their charm loadouts.
